## Common Content Patterns & Themes

### Technology Education (Found in 8/10 pages)
- **HEPA Filter Technology**: Mentioned on CellphoneS, Dyson, Airdog, Daikin
  - "Lọc 99.97% bụi PM2.5" messaging
  - Filter longevity emphasis
  
- **Activated Carbon**: Highlighted by CellphoneS, Airdog
  - Odor elimination focus
  - Formaldehyde absorption claims

- **Smart/IoT Features**: Dyson, Daikin, Panasonic
  - App control
  - Air quality monitoring
  - Auto-adjustment capabilities

### Problem-Solution Framework
**Problem**: Vietnam's air quality crisis
- PM2.5 6x higher than WHO recommendations
- Seasonal pollution peaks (Oct-March)
- Urban health concerns (Hanoi, HCMC especially)

**Solution Messaging** (consistent across competitors):
- Fast delivery/installation
- Genuine product guarantees
- Warranty coverage
- After-sales support emphasis

### Buyer Journey Touchpoints
1. **Awareness**: "Tại sao cần máy lọc không khí?"
2. **Education**: Technology comparison, brand backgrounds
3. **Decision**: Price comparison, feature matrices, warranty info
4. **Purchase**: Multiple payment options (installment, discounts)
5. **Post-Purchase**: Service registration, filter replacement schedules
