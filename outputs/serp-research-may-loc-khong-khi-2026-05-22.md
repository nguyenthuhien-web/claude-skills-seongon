## SECTION 4: WINNING FORMAT RECOMMENDATIONS

### Recommended Content Type: **Hybrid Listicle + Educational Guide**

**Why This Works:**
- Combines high engagement (listicle) with authority building (guide)
- Addresses multiple search intents simultaneously
- Natural product integration opportunities
- Better shareability than pure guides
- Higher conversion potential than pure comparisons

**Recommended Structure (3,500-4,000 words):**
1. **Hook/Problem Statement** (150 words)
   - Air quality crisis in Vietnam
   - Health impact statistics
   - Reader pain point identification

2. **Educational Section** (600 words)
   - What is air purifier technology
   - How HEPA filters work
   - CADR explained
   - Key selection criteria

3. **Buying Guide/Criteria** (700 words)
   - Room size calculation
   - Noise level importance (bedroom vs living room)
   - Filter type comparison
   - Price brackets (budget/mid/premium)
   - Energy efficiency considerations

4. **Top 7-10 Products** (2,000 words)
   - 150-200 word reviews each
   - Spec comparison table
   - Pros/cons bullets
   - Price information
   - Best for (specific use cases)

5. **FAQ Section** (300 words)
   - Common questions answered
   - Maintenance schedule
   - Warranty information
   - Return policies

6. **CTA Section** (100-150 words)
   - Clear next steps
   - Multiple CTA options
   - Risk reversal messaging

### SEO Optimization Strategy:
- **Primary keyword:** Máy lọc không khí Việt Nam (position in title, H1, first 100 words)
- **Long-tail keywords:** "cách chọn máy lọc không khí," "máy lọc không khí tốt nhất," "giá máy lọc không khí"
- **Semantic keywords:** HEPA, CADR, filter replacement, air quality, efficiency, noise level, maintenance
- **Internal linking:** Link to related content on related topics
- **Media:** Include 2-3 comparison tables, 1-2 embedded videos, product images
