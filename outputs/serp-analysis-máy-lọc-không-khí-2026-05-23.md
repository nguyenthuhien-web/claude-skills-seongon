## Common Patterns in Top 10

### 1. **Title Formula**
**Pattern:** [Product Name] + [Key Benefit/Feature] + [Brand/Source]

**Examples:**
- "Máy lọc không khí | Máy lọc HEPA | Dyson VN" (Format: Product | Feature | Brand)
- "Máy lọc không khí chính hãng GIẢM 46% | Hỏa tốc 1H" (Format: Product | Discount | Offer)
- "Máy lọc không khí giá tốt | Bụi mịn, diệt khuẩn, HEPA | Karcher" (Format: Product | Benefits | Brand)

**Key Elements:**
- Include "chính hãng" (authentic/genuine) for credibility
- Highlight discount percentage or special offer (GIẢM 46%, giá bán buôn)
- Feature HEPA technology (most mentioned benefit)
- Geo-specific (Vietnam markets, quick delivery)

### 2. **Content Structure**
**Most Common Layouts:**
1. **Brand Sites (Dyson, LG, Panasonic, Karcher):** Product showcase → Comparison → Educational content
2. **Major Retailers (CellphoneS, HC):** Product listing → Buying guide + FAQs → Technology education
3. **Specialist Retailers:** Directory listing → Product showcase → Contact/support

### 3. **Content Depth**
- **Shallow:** Specialist/wholesale sites (1,500-2,000 words) - focus on listing
- **Medium:** Brand sites with basic comparison (2,500-3,000 words)
- **Deep:** Major retailers with buying guides (8,500-9,000 words) - CellphoneS is standout

### 4. **Featured Content Elements**

| Element | Frequency | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Product Comparison Table | 60% | Product differentiation |
| Buying Guide / Selection Criteria | 50% | User education |
| Technology Explanations | 50% | Trust building |
| Price/Discount Highlighting | 80% | Conversion |
| FAQ Section | 40% | User objection handling |
| Interactive Tools (Quiz) | 20% | Engagement |
| Brand Filters | 30% | Navigation |

### 5. **CTA Strategy**
**Primary CTAs (100% coverage):**
- Mua ngay / Đặt hàng (Buy Now / Order) - direct commerce
- Contact/Support links - trust & assistance

**Secondary CTAs (60% coverage):**
- Trả góp / Installment options - affordability
- Tìm hiểu thêm (Learn More) - relationship building

**Engagement CTAs (20-30% coverage):**
- Registration/Newsletter signup
- Help/Quiz tools
- Wishlist/Comparison features

### 6. **Heading Hierarchy**

**Typical Structure:**
1. **H1:** Keyword-focused main heading (e.g., "Máy lọc không khí")
2. **H2:** Category/benefit-focused (e.g., "Máy lọc HEPA", "Công nghệ lọc không khí")
3. **H3:** Detailed specifications/comparisons

### 7. **Target Audience Signals**
- **B2C Focus:** 80% (retailers, brand sites with direct purchase)
- **B2B Focus:** 10% (wholesale sites like banhangtaikho.com.vn)
- **Mixed:** 10% (educational sites serving both)

## Content Gap Opportunities

### **Gaps Found in Current SERP:**
1. **Missing:** In-depth air purifier comparison guides (side-by-side brands)
   - Only 1-2 sites do true brand comparisons (Dyson mostly self-comparison)
   
2. **Missing:** Health/medical angle content
   - No content specifically about asthma, allergies, air quality impact on health
   - No content about WHO air quality guidelines for Vietnam
   
3. **Missing:** Installation & maintenance guides
   - No dedicated content about filter replacement, cleaning, upkeep
   
4. **Missing:** Room-size specific buying guides
   - No content about "máy lọc không khí phòng ngủ 20m2" or specific room guidance
   
5. **Missing:** Cost-of-ownership analysis
   - No content comparing total cost including filters, electricity, maintenance

## Recommended Content Strategy

### **Primary Recommendation:**
Create a **"Comprehensive Air Purifier Buying Guide"** with:
- **Target Word Count:** 8,000-10,000 words
- **Structure:**
  1. What are air purifiers? (Technology overview)
  2. Key technologies explained (HEPA, activated carbon, UV-C, ionization)
  3. How to choose by room size/needs (Decision framework)
  4. Top brands in Vietnam market comparison (Table format)
  5. Best models by budget tier (Recommended selections)
  6. Installation & maintenance guide
  7. Health benefits & scientific backing
  8. FAQs about warranties, returns, support
  9. Buying checklist

### **Secondary Opportunity:**
Create specialized guides for niches missing from SERP:
- "Máy lọc không khí cho người bị dị ứng" (Air purifiers for allergies)
- "Máy lọc không khí phòng ngủ: Hướng dẫn chọn" (Bedroom air purifiers guide)
- "Chi phí vận hành máy lọc không khí: Tính toán chi tiết" (Operating cost analysis)

## Summary

The **"máy lọc không khí"** keyword SERP is dominated by:
1. **Brand sites** (Dyson, LG, Panasonic) - leveraging domain authority
2. **Major retailers** (CellphoneS, HC) - combining product listings with educational content
